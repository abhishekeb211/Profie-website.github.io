## Executive Overview

**SIH Swasthya** is an integrated public health intelligence and clinical operations platform engineered to digitize frontline primary healthcare delivery across rural, tribal, and peri-urban India.

Developed in alignment with statutory **National Health Mission (NHM)** directives, the Indian Public Health Standards (IPHS), and Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Center mandates, Swasthya digitizes **236 statutory health worker duties** across **21 national disease control programs** with offline-first field synchronization and epidemiological outbreak forecasting.

## Frontline Cadre Matrix & Workflows

1. **ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife / Arogya Sevika):** Directs reproductive, maternal, neonatal, and child health (RMNCH+A), monitoring pregnant mothers across 4 mandatory Antenatal Care (ANC) checkups, automated High-Risk Pregnancy (HRP) triage, and cold-chain vaccine logistics.
2. **MPW-M (Multi-Purpose Worker Male / Arogya Sevak):** Active surveillance for vector-borne diseases (Malaria slide examination, Dengue larval search), non-communicable disease (NCD) population enumeration via CBAC forms, and Nikshay tuberculosis sputum collection.
3. **ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist):** Community mobilization, institutional delivery facilitation, and village health sanitation committees.
