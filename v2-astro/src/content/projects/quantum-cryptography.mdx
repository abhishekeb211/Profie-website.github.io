## Executive Overview

This scientific research asset comprises an in-depth mathematical monograph and empirical investigation into **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)** and **Quantum-Resistant Federated Learning (QRFL)** designed for blockchain-enabled healthcare consortia.

With quantum computing advances threatening classical asymmetric cryptosystems (RSA, ECDSA, Diffie-Hellman), this work analyzes how lattice-based cryptographic primitives maintain confidentiality and Byzantine consensus across distributed hospital nodes.

## Core Mathematical Formulations

The security of the proposed QRFL framework rests on the hardness of the **Decisional Learning With Errors (LWE)** problem:
Given a public matrix `(A) in Z_q^(m x n)` and a vector `(b) in Z_q^m`, distinguish between:
```
(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{s} + \mathbf{e} \pmod q) \quad \text{and} \quad (\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{u} \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{Z}_q^m)
```
where `(s) in Z_q^n` is a secret vector and `(e) in chi^m` is a small error vector drawn from a discrete Gaussian distribution.

When the lattice dimension `n >= 512`, no known classical or quantum algorithm can solve LWE in sub-exponential time, providing long-term forward secrecy for clinical records.
