## Executive Summary & Research Contribution

The **Multi-Cross CNN-Transformer-ViT (MC-CTViT)** platform is an empirical affective computing framework designed to classify human emotional states from multi-channel Electroencephalogram (EEG) biosignals. It evaluates affective dynamics across three international benchmark corpora:
- **DEAP:** 32-channel preprocessed EEG recorded during music video stimulation.
- **SEED:** 62-channel 3-session clinical EEG during film clip exposure.
- **MAHNOB-HCI:** 32-channel variable-length multimodal affective trials.

Unlike conventional monolithic deep networks that flatten multi-channel biosignals into an undifferentiated token sequence, MC-CTViT captures the heterogeneous physical characteristics of brain activity through four specialized architectural branches.

## Architectural Quad-Branch Mechanics

```
                       Raw Multi-Channel EEG (B, C, F, T)
                                       │
     ┌──────────────────┬──────────────┴─────────────┬─────────────────┐
     ▼                  ▼                            ▼                 ▼
Multi-Scale 1D CNN   Temporal Transformer    Spatial Transformer   2D Scalp Energy
(Receptive Fields    (Long-Range Attention   (+ Phase-Locking      Topographic Map
  k = 3, 5, 7)        over DE Sub-windows)     Value Matrices)            │
     │                  │                            │             Vision Transformer
     │                  │                            │             (4x4 Patch Embed)
     └──────────────────┼────────────────────────────┼─────────────────┘
                        ▼                            ▼
                 Multi-Cross Attention Fusion Layer (Native Sequence Lengths)
                                       │
                     Multitask Classification Heads
                        ├── Valence (High / Low / 3-Class)
                        └── Arousal (High / Low / Quadrants)
```

### 1. Multi-Scale 1D CNN Branch
Parallel convolution kernels with GELU activations capture transient micro-oscillations, sleep spindles, and sharp potentials across receptive field windows (`k in (3, 5, 7)`).

### 2. Temporal Transformer Branch
Models long-range temporal dependencies across sub-window Differential Entropy (DE) frames extracted across standard physiological bands (`delta, theta, alpha, beta, gamma`).

### 3. Spatial Transformer & Phase Locking Value (PLV)
Interprets electrode channels as discrete spatial tokens projected into latent dimension `d_(model)`, enriched with Phase Locking Value (PLV) functional connectivity matrices:
```
\text{PLV}_{ij} = \frac{1}{N} \left| \sum_{n=1}^N \exp(i(\theta_{i,n} - \theta_{j,n})) \right|
```

### 4. Vision Transformer on Topographic Scalp Projections
Azimuthal equidistant projection maps 3D electrode positions onto a 2D interpolated cranial energy plane (`32 x 32`), tokenized into `4 x 4` patches. This enables self-attention over hemispheric frontal alpha asymmetry without spatial distortion.

## 99.9% Attention Computational Reduction

In monolithic transformers, self-attention operates over joint sequence length `N = C x T`. For SEED (`C=62, T=60`), `N=3,720`, yielding `~= 1.38 x 10^7` attention operations per head. 

MC-CTViT decouples temporal (`T=60`) from spatial (`C=62`) self-attention, reducing computational complexity to:
```
\mathcal{O}(C^2 \cdot d + T^2 \cdot d) \approx \mathcal{O}((62^2 + 60^2) \cdot d) = \mathcal{O}(7,444 \cdot d)
```

This achieves a **theoretical compute reduction exceeding 99.9%** in attention calculation, allowing training and low-latency inference on standard desktop GPU hardware.
