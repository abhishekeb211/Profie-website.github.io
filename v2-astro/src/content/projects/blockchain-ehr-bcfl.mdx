## Executive Context & Healthcare Imperative
Centralized electronic health records (EHR) represent catastrophic single points of failure, exposing patient data to unauthorized monetization and vendor breaches. Simultaneously, hospitals cannot pool raw patient records to train diagnostic machine learning models due to HIPAA and GDPR restrictions. **Blockchain-EHR & BCFL-POCC** solves both dilemmas through decentralized cryptographic sovereignty.

## Algorithmic & Byzantine Resistance: Proof-of-Convergence (PoCC)
In standard Federated Averaging (FedAvg), malicious or faulty nodes can poison the global model by submitting divergent weights. Under the **Proof-of-Convergence (PoCC)** consensus algorithm, an update `Delta W_i` is committed to the ledger if and only if it satisfies:
```
\|\nabla \mathcal{L}(W_{global} + \Delta W_i) - \nabla \mathcal{L}(W_{global})\|_2 \le \gamma \cdot \|\Delta W_i\|_2
```
where `gamma` is the Lipschitz continuity constant of the clinical diagnostic objective function. Divergent updates are discarded before on-chain weight commitment.
