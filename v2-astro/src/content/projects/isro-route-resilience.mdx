## Executive Context & Hackathon Directive
Engineered for the **ISRO Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon (BAH 2026)**, this platform addresses post-disaster emergency logistics. Floods, landslides, and cyclones frequently rupture critical arterial transport networks. Optical satellite imagery (Cartosat, Sentinel) experiences severe visual fragmentation from cloud shadows, mud inundation, and fallen tree canopies, crippling manual GIS routing teams.

## Algorithmic & Mathematical Deductions

1. **Angle-Aware Directional Gap Scoring:** Standard nearest-neighbor gap closing creates catastrophic false links across parallel city streets. Two dead-end nodes `u, v` with headings `vec(d)_u, vec(d)_v` and distance `L = |u - v|_2` are bridged via:
```
\text{Score}(u, v) = L \cdot \left(1 + \lambda (1 - \cos \theta_u) + \lambda (1 - \cos \theta_v)\right)
```
   where `theta_u` is the deflection angle between `vec(d)_u` and `(v - u)`, heavily penalizing unnatural angular breaks.
2. **Global Network Resilience Index (`R`):** Evaluates overall network transport efficiency `mathcal(E)(G)` under simulated inundation buffers:
```
\mathcal{E}(G) = \frac{1}{N(N-1)} \sum_{i \ne j} \frac{1}{d(i, j)}, \quad R = \frac{\mathcal{E}(G_{flood})}{\mathcal{E}(G_{baseline})}
```
   Providing disaster response commanders with direct quantitative measurements of transport degradation.
