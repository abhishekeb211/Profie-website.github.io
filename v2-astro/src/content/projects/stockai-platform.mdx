## Executive Overview

**StockAI** is an enterprise-grade quantitative market research and distributed financial intelligence platform. Designed to process voluminous real-time tick streams, technical indicators, and financial news disclosures, it pairs high-performance **TimescaleDB hypertables** with an asynchronous **Celery worker cluster** to generate quantitative sentiment scores and risk metrics.

## Core Engineering Highlights

### 1. TimescaleDB Time-Series Optimization
Utilizes native PostgreSQL extensions with chunk-interval hypertable partitioning. Analytical queries over multi-year OHLCV candles execute with indexed sub-millisecond efficiency.

### 2. Distributed Worker Orchestration
Celery worker pools with Celery Beat schedulers decouple real-time socket ingestion from compute-intensive machine learning inferences, preventing market feed drops during volatility spikes.

### 3. Containerized Enterprise Deployment
Packaged with multi-service `docker-compose.yml` defining automated database healthcheck probes, Redis persistent volumes, and auto-restart policies.
