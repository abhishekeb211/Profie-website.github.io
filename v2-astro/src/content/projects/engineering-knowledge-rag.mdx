## Context & Problem
Standard naive vector retrieval often fails on complex engineering manuals and technical schematics because keyword exactness (part numbers, error codes) is diluted by semantic embeddings.

## Engineering Architecture
1. **Hybrid Ingestion:** Dual-indexing pipeline generating BM25 inverted indexes for exact identifiers and dense HNSW embeddings for conceptual queries.
2. **Cross-Encoder Reranking:** Top 50 candidates are rescored using a cross-encoder to capture semantic nuance and cross-sentence dependencies.
3. **Citation Provenance Engine:** Answers are compiled with strict citation boundaries; sentences lacking reference evidence are automatically redacted.

## Provenance & Evaluation
- Validated across mechanical engineering handbooks and academic syllabi.
- Significant reduction in hallucinations compared to naive vector search baselines.
