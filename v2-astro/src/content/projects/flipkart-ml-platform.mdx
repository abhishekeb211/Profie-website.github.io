## Executive Context & Retail Scale
E-commerce retail platforms handle millions of diverse SKUs where naive one-hot encoding leads to memory-saturating sparse matrices, and flat regression models fail to account for differing demand elasticity between budget commodity items and luxury goods. This platform addresses large-scale retail pricing prediction and customer demand forecasting.

## Algorithmic Innovations & Segmented Modeling

1. **High-Cardinality Categorical Handling:** Rather than naive one-hot encoding, the system leverages CatBoost's **Ordered Target Statistics** to encode high-cardinality brand and category features without causing target leakage.
2. **Segmented Regression Ensembles:** Decomposes heterogeneous product catalogs into distinct price and demand tiers, training specialized regression sub-models for premium vs commodity goods.
3. **Comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis:** Full statistical profiling tracking skewness, mutual information scores, and non-linear feature interactions.

## Empirical Verification
- **Packaging:** Modular inference scripts, trained gradient-boosted decision trees, and reproducible validation pipelines.
- **Maturity:** Tier 3 Hardened Machine Learning Regression Platform.
